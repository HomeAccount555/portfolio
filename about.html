About me

Hi, I'm Yunhe, but most people call me Henry.

I'm a Bachelor of Arts student in Interactive Arts & Technology (Co-op) at Simon Fraser University's School of Interactive Arts & Technology (SIAT). I started at SFU in 2024 in Computing Science and moved to SIAT in 2026, and I expect to graduate in 2029.

That path shapes how I work. From Computing Science I kept programming, data structures and databases. At SIAT I'm adding visual design and user-centred thinking. I like projects where both halves matter: a system has to be well organized underneath, and it has to be clear to the person using it.

I'm also comfortable keeping a team moving. On both projects in this portfolio I organized meetings, tracked what was done and what wasn't, and presented our work.
